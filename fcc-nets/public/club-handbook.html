Part 1 · Structure

How the club is run

Power in FCC flows from the members. The members elect the board at the Annual General Meeting. The board sets direction and appoints committee chairs. Committees do the work, with helpers from across the club.

Highest authority Members · Annual General Meeting Every paid-up member over 18 votes (one vote each). Elects the board, the auditor and the selection committee, approves the accounts and budget, and changes the statutes.
Independent of the boardAuditor: checks the accounts.Selection committee: picks captains and squads.Both are elected by, and report to, the AGM.Advisory Council: former chairs and founders advise and mediate, with no vote.

ChairmanVice ChairmanTreasurerSecretaryCommittee chairs with a board seat
Appointed by the board Committees Each committee has a chair, a written purpose and a budget. The chair recruits members and helpers.
YouthMatchesCoachingFinance & SponsorshipComms & GrowthEquipment & GroundsEvents & Social
Everyone else Captains, coaches, helpers and members The One FCC Lead and Co-Lead develop the club's framework. Captains run teams on match day. Coaches run training. Every member is expected to help with at least one task a season.

Separation of roles

The Club keeps four jobs apart: governance (the Board), player selection (the selection committee), coaching (the coaching committee) and team leadership (captains). No one holds two of these at once, so every decision is fair, transparent and accountable. Any temporary exception must be approved and recorded: by the selection chair for selection matters, or by the Board for governance matters.

Part 3 · Applies to everyone

Code of conduct

These standards apply to every member, player, parent, coach, captain and board member, on and off the field, in person and in our WhatsApp groups. Joining FCC means agreeing to them.

RespectTeammates, opponents, umpires, volunteers and neighbours.
Fair playPlay within the Laws and the Spirit of Cricket, and accept the umpire's decision.
InclusionEvery nationality, age, gender and ability is welcome.
CommitmentTurn up when you say you will. Tell us early when you can't.
ContributionThe club runs on volunteers. Everyone gives something back.
DignityPraise in public, correct in private. No one is embarrassed in front of others.

Every member will

  • Pay membership fees by the deadline set each season
  • Reply to availability requests by the captain's deadline
  • Take on at least one helper task per season, from scoring a game to helping at an event
  • Look after club kit and return it in the condition it was lent
  • Raise disagreements privately with the person concerned, or with the relevant chair
  • Follow the club's safeguarding rules around children
  • Keep selection, disciplinary and personal matters confidential

No member will

  • Abuse, threaten or harass anyone, in person or online
  • Discriminate by nationality, ethnicity, language, region, religion, caste, gender, age or ability
  • Argue with or show dissent towards an umpire
  • Criticise teammates, captains or volunteers in group chats
  • Drink alcohol at youth sessions or before playing
  • Post photos of children without parental consent
  • Make public statements or posts that damage the Club's reputation

WhatsApp and social media

All club groups
  • Club groups are for club business: training, matches, events and announcements.
  • Festival greetings are welcome. Political or religious debate, chain forwards and advertising are not.
  • Club groups use English (or Danish), so every member can follow.
  • Disagree in private. A complaint posted to a group is handled as a conduct issue.
  • Group admins may remove messages and, after one warning, members.
  • Only the Comms committee posts on the club's official social media.
  • Member contact details stay inside the club and are never shared outside it.

Community & inclusion

How we look after each other

FCC brings together members from many countries, languages, faiths and generations. These everyday rules make sure everyone feels at home.

Calendar, food and kit

  • The club calendar marks major festivals such as Diwali, Eid, Holi and Christmas. The AGM and club events avoid those dates where possible
  • Training times take account of fasting periods where they can
  • Every club event offers a vegetarian option. Food is labelled: vegetarian, non-vegetarian, halal, beef or pork, and common allergens
  • Modest kit (long sleeves, leggings, head coverings) is always fine, as long as it is safe to play in

Language and respect

  • Club groups and documents use English, with Danish for official matters, so every member can follow. Private chats can be in any language
  • We respect experience, but decisions are made on merit and due process, not seniority
  • Women and girls get equal access to nets, kit and coaching time
  • New members and families are welcomed personally, not just added to a group

Part 4 · Governance

The board

Board of Directors

Elected by the AGM2-year terms

The board holds the club in trust for its members. It runs the club, not the teams and players. It sets direction, controls the money, and delegates the day-to-day work to committees.

Size5 to 9 members, ideally an odd number
Core seatsChairman, Vice Chairman, Treasurer, Secretary
MeetsAt least every 6 to 8 weeks
Accountable toThe members, at the AGM

Who can stand

  • A member aged 18 or over who has paid this season's fees
  • A member for at least one full season before standing
  • Holds a valid child certificate (børneattest) if the role involves youth
  • Not currently a team captain or vice-captain
  • Not a member of the selection committee
  • Not related to, or living with, the auditor

Terms and elections

  • Terms are 2 years and staggered, so about half the board is elected each year
  • A member may serve at most 2 consecutive terms in the same officer role, then step out of it for one term (proposed, from the 2027 AGM)
  • A vacancy between AGMs is filled by the board until the next AGM
  • A member who misses 3 meetings in a row without notice is treated as having resigned

How the board meets

  • The agenda and papers go out 7 days before each meeting
  • Quorum is more than half the board, including the Chairman or Vice Chairman
  • Decisions are made by simple majority. The Chairman has the casting vote on a tie
  • A summary of decisions is shared with members within 14 days
  • Each committee chair gives a short written update before every meeting

Board member duties

  • Act in the interest of the whole club, not one team or group
  • Declare any conflict of interest and leave the room for that item
  • Support board decisions in public once they are made (collective responsibility)
  • Keep confidential matters confidential, especially about individuals
  • Delegate to committees rather than doing their work for them

AGM, elections & voting

Fair, predictable elections

Elections should be decided by the members who are genuinely part of the club, with no surprises on the night.

Before the AGM

  • Notice and agenda go out as the statutes require
  • Nominations for the board and the selection committee close 14 days before the AGM and are published to members (proposed). Nominations on the night are taken only for a seat with no candidate
  • Proposals from members for the agenda are submitted in writing by the deadline in the statutes

Who votes, and how

  • Members aged 18+ who have been members for at least 3 months, with fees paid at least 30 days before the AGM (proposed). This stops last-minute sign-ups swinging a vote
  • One member, one vote. Parents of youth players vote only if they are members themselves
  • Proxy votes only if the statutes allow them
  • Secret ballot for any contested election, or whenever a member asks for one

Advisory Council & honorary members

Experience without authority

The people who built FCC keep an honoured place in it. Their experience stays with the club, without anyone having to hold a role for ever.

Who and what

  • Former chairmen, founders and long-serving members, invited by the board
  • Advise the board when asked, and keep the club's history
  • Act as neutral mediators in disputes (Part 11)
  • Take ceremonial roles, such as presenting awards at awards night

The Council does not

  • Vote on board decisions or direct committees
  • Influence selection
Honorary life membership may be awarded by the AGM for outstanding service to the club.

Part 6 · Getting things done

How every committee works

Committees are where the club's work happens. All of them follow the same simple rules, so members know what to expect wherever they help.

Plan & organise
A one-page charter

Each committee writes down its purpose, scope, members (2 to 6 people), season goals and budget request. The board approves it.

Lead & recruit
A named chair who builds a team

The board appoints the chair for one season, renewable. The one exception is the selection committee, which the AGM elects. The chair recruits committee members and one-off helpers, and no one person holds more than two chair roles.

Approve & execute
Decide within scope, report back

The committee decides anything within its charter and budget. It sends a short update before each board meeting and escalates anything outside its scope.

End of season
Review and hand over

The chair reports what worked, what didn't and what's needed next year, and leaves a handover note for whoever follows.

Committee chair: role & code

Applies to every chairSigned appointment

Every committee chair owns one area of the club, builds a team around it, and is accountable for delivering it.

Responsibilities

  • Writes and keeps the committee's charter: purpose, scope, goals and budget
  • Recruits committee members and helpers, and delegates real tasks to them
  • Spends only within the committee budget and the limits in Part 10
  • Sends a short written update before each board meeting
  • Keeps simple notes of committee decisions
  • Leaves a handover note at the end of the season

Code

  • Acts in the interest of the whole club
  • Declares any conflict of interest and steps out of that decision
  • Decides only within the committee's scope, and escalates anything outside it
  • Respects the remit of other committees, the selection committee in particular
  • Includes new members, rather than keeping the work in a small circle

Part 7 · Committee framework

Selection committee

Selection Committee

New for 2027Elected by the AGMChair + 4 selectors

The selection committee picks players and team leaders in a fair, transparent and consistent way. It supports both winning cricket and the Club's long-term development, and it acts independently, objectively and in the Club's best interests.

MembersChair + 4 selectors, one per division
DecidesSquads, captains and vice-captains
Does not decideThe playing XI. That is the captain's call
CoachesGive input, but have no vote
CaptainsShare team needs. The final decision is the committee's and must be respected
Elected byThe AGM, and independent of the board

What the committee is responsible for

Player selection

Picks each team's squad on agreed principles: performance, ability, availability, commitment, development, fitness and team needs. The captain then picks the XI from the squad.

Team balance

Builds balanced, competitive teams with the right mix of batting, bowling, wicketkeeping, fielding and leadership.

Captains and vice-captains

Asks for proposals from players who want to captain, considers all of them, then appoints on leadership, experience, conduct, commitment and suitability.

Player development

Weighs long-term development in every decision, with real chances for emerging and developing players.

Consistent selection

Applies the same principles across all teams and players. No arbitrary decisions or personal preferences.

Availability and commitment

Takes account of availability, training attendance, commitment to the Club and reliability.

Communication

Tells players, captains and coaches about decisions clearly, respectfully and on time.

Confidentiality

Keeps discussions, individual assessments, player information and committee deliberations confidential.

Selection records

Records selections, and the reasons behind significant decisions, for consistency and accountability.

Review and adjust

Reviews team make-up and player development during the season, and adjusts for performance, availability, development and changing team needs.

Selection committee policy

Independence & fairness

Who cannot be a selector

  • Board members. This keeps governance and selection independent
  • Captains of any FCC team, and vice-captains
  • Anyone who wants to be considered for captaincy that season

Conflicts of interest

  • A selector takes no part in decisions about their own selection, playing position or team placement
  • Any actual, possible or perceived conflict, including a family member, is declared to the committee, and the selector withdraws from that decision
  • No gifts, favours or pressure from players or families. Any attempt is reported to the selection chair

How decisions are made

  • Collectively, by the committee, never by one person
  • Independently, free from pressure by board members, captains, coaches, players or other members
  • Without favouritism: friendships, family, disagreements, seniority, nationality and social ties play no part
  • Supported once made. Selectors back a collective decision and never undermine it in public or in a team

Feedback and changes

  • Each decision is announced with a short reason
  • A player can ask the chair for feedback within 7 days, and gets a reply within 7 more
  • Moving a player between teams mid-season: the committee, after hearing from the captains involved
  • A captain steps down or is removed: a replacement is appointed within 7 days
  • Complaints about the process go to the board, which can ask the committee to re-run it but cannot change a selection decision
  • A vacancy between AGMs is filled by the committee, and announced to members
Why independent: keeping selection outside the board removes any conflict, or appearance of collusion. The board runs the club, and the selection committee picks the teams. Separation of roles: the selection committee decides who is selected and who leads each team. Captains lead and manage the selected team in matches. Coaches support player development and performance.

Governance & independence

Captaincy policy

Accepting the captaincy is a commitment to lead the team independently and fairly, in the Club's best interests. These rules keep roles clear and avoid any real or perceived conflict of interest.

Independence

  • From selection. A serving captain is not on the selection committee. The captain may tell the committee what type of player, or which specific player, the team needs. The committee's final decision must be respected
  • From the Board. A serving captain does not sit on the Board. The Board governs and oversees, and the captain carries out team-level decisions within that framework
  • No personal interest. A captain takes no part in decisions about their own selection, team placement, playing position, or anything else they have a direct personal interest in

Fairness and trust

  • No retaliation or favouritism. A captain never uses the position to favour, disadvantage, exclude or retaliate against a player over personal disagreements, selection decisions or other non-sporting matters
  • Confidentiality. Selection, disciplinary, personal and confidential Club matters stay within the proper Club structures
  • Temporary exceptions. Any temporary exception to these rules must be approved by the selection chair (selection matters) or the Board (governance matters), and recorded

Team leadership & player management

Before and during the season

The captain creates a fair, well-organised team where every player knows their role and has a real chance to contribute.

Before the season

  • Clear roles. Every player knows their expected batting position, bowling and fielding responsibilities, and match-day duties
  • Clear communication. Roles are shared before the season. Changes are explained before the match wherever possible, so players can prepare
  • Fair opportunities. With the selection and coaching committees, make sure squad players get fair, meaningful chances across the season, based on availability, performance, development and team needs

Throughout the season

  • No personal favouritism. Roles, chances and match decisions are never shaped by friendships, disagreements or other non-sporting reasons
  • Develop players. Give players chances to take responsibility, try new roles and grow
  • Equal respect. Every player is treated the same, whatever their batting position, role, seniority or experience
  • Constructive feedback. Give honest, constructive feedback on performance and behaviour, and keep team communication open and respectful

Match-week framework

Early in the week
Availability poll

Sent in the team group or app, with a clear reply deadline.

At least 48 hrs before
XI and duty roster announced

Playing XI, plus the scorer, ground set-up, tea and transport duties. Every player takes a turn, the captain included. A dropped player hears it from the captain in private, with the reason, before the XI is published.

Match day
Lead the team

Arrive 1 hour before start. Greet the opposition and umpires, and make sure umpiring and scoring duties are covered. The captain sets the field, bowling changes, batting order and tactics, within the Club's framework.

Within 24 hrs after
Close the match

Scorecard submitted to DCF and kit returned. A short match report goes to the Matches chair: result, key observations, player contributions, notable incidents and anything needing follow-up. Any significant disciplinary, behaviour, safety, injury, umpiring or player issue is reported promptly to the Board.

Part 10 · Financial controls

Money rules

Clear limits protect the club and the volunteers handling its money. No one should ever be the only person who can see or move the club's money.

Spend (DKK, proposed)Who approvesCondition
Up to 1,000Committee chairWithin the committee's budget
1,001 – 5,000Committee chair + TreasurerWithin budget, logged
Over 5,000Board decisionRecorded in the minutes
Anything not in the budgetBoard decisionRecorded in the minutes, with a funding source
  • Two people approve every bank payment above 5,000 DKK (dual approval in online banking)
  • At least two board members can see the bank account at all times
  • Reimbursements need a receipt and are claimed within 30 days
  • No cash without a receipt. Event cash is counted by two people
  • Sponsorship deals over 10,000 DKK are signed off by the board
  • Buying from a member's or relative's business (catering, kit, printing) is declared first, and needs two quotes above 5,000 DKK
  • Fee reductions or hardship support follow published rules (such as a family discount), are approved by the Treasurer and Chairman, and stay confidential
  • Members can ask to see the approved budget at any time
  • All club money goes into club accounts, including the club MobilePay. Never into a personal account, not even temporarily

Part 11 · When things go wrong

Complaints & discipline

Most issues are solved with a conversation. When they aren't, this stepped process makes sure everyone is treated the same way and gets a fair hearing.

1Informal wordThe captain, coach or committee chair talks to the member calmly and privately. A short note is kept. Captains escalate serious or unresolved matters to the selection committee or the Chairman.
MMediation (optional)If both sides agree, a neutral, respected member, for example from the Advisory Council, helps them settle it.

Part 12 · Club development

One FCC: Club Development & Coordination

Someone has to build and look after this framework. The Board gives that job a formal mandate, so members know who is developing the Club's structures, and on whose authority.

Club Development & Coordination Lead

Lead + Co-LeadAppointed by the BoardAppointment letter

Helps FCC grow from an informally run club into a structured, transparent, well-documented and member-driven one, with clear responsibilities, processes and accountability.

HoldersA Lead and a Co-Lead, who deputises and shares the mandate
Reports toThe Chairman, following any collective Board decision
Board member?No, and no vote on Board decisions
TermUntil the Board ends it. The holder may step down with 7 days' notice

Responsibilities

  • Governance framework: propose how the Board, committees, working groups, coaches and captains relate, and who holds what authority
  • Committee development: help set up and run committees, including their purpose, leadership and reporting
  • Policies and procedures: draft policies, codes of conduct, role descriptions, appointment letters and procedures
  • Roles and responsibilities: set clear expectations for board members, committee leads, captains, coaches and volunteers
  • Operational improvement: find practical ways to make club activities more organised, efficient and transparent

 

  • Documentation: make sure key decisions, roles, processes and policies are written down, so the Club depends less on what individuals know
  • Implementation support: once approved, coordinate putting changes into practice across committees and teams
  • Member participation: create ways for more members to give their skills, experience and time
  • Continuous development: review how well the structures work and recommend improvements to the Board
  • Strategic initiatives: support Club-wide projects when the Board asks

Mandate from the Board

  • Develop and propose new structures, policies, procedures, committees and frameworks
  • Consult board members, committee leads, captains, coaches and members
  • Coordinate agreed frameworks and support their roll-out
  • Recommend directly to the Board on governance, organisation, policy and operations
  • Implement approved changes
  • Access the club information and documents the role reasonably needs, within confidentiality rules

Limits of authority: cannot alone

  • Make decisions reserved for the Board or the AGM
  • Select players or teams
  • Appoint captains or vice-captains
  • Override the selection or coaching committees
  • Commit the Club to spending or contracts without approval
  • Change club policies without approval from the right body

The role develops, coordinates, recommends and supports. It has no authority over any committee. The Board gives direction, considers proposals, approves changes within its authority, may assign projects, and may invite the Lead or Co-Lead to Board or committee meetings. Because the role is not on the Board or the selection committee, its holders may hold other club roles where there is no conflict. Any actual or possible conflict is declared to the Board.

Principles

TransparencyClear, understandable processes.
ParticipationMore members contributing.
AccountabilityClear ownership for everything.
ConsistencyCommon standards across teams and committees.
CollaborationWork constructively with everyone.
Continuous improvementPractical changes that make the Club stronger.
Club firstThe Club's long-term interest over any person, team or group.
Recommended: a short progress update to members each quarter, and a review of the role at each AGM, in line with the rest of this handbook. Changes to the mandate are agreed in writing.
